Why Cavite

Close to Metro Manila, and getting closer

To Pasay, PITX & NAIA

Via CAVITEX. Closest from Tanza, which is linked to CAVITEX and CALAX.

To Alabang

Via Daang Hari from General Trias, or CALAX to SLEX.

To Tagaytay

Via Governor's Drive and Aguinaldo Highway through Dasmariñas. The CTBEX expressway is upcoming.

Travel time depends on traffic. Ask me for directions from where you work.

Road and transport projects around the three locations:

  • CALAX final phaseLinks to CAVITEX via the Open Canal interchange; eases traffic on Aguinaldo and Arnaldo highways.
  • Gentri-Dasma Diversion Road openConnects Dasmariñas to General Trias; travel time cut from about 30 to 10 minutes.
  • LRT Line 6 proposedCommuter rail from Niyog, Bacoor to Dasmariñas along Aguinaldo Highway.
  • Sangley Point International Airport upcomingNew international gateway in Cavite City.
  • Cavite–Bataan Interlink Bridge plannedBridge across Manila Bay, projected for 2029.
  • CAVITEX & PITXEasy commute to Pasay and Metro Manila.

How to reserve

Three steps to your home

  1. 1
    Choose your unit

    Join an online presentation or a site viewing. I'll show you the available lots and send your updated computation.

  2. 2
    Pay the reservation fee

    ₱15,000 for Beatrice SF, Bettina and Alecza · ₱10,000 for Elena. Pay through the developer's official channels: credit or debit card, e-wallet, online banking or over the counter.

  3. 3
    Submit your documents

    Send the requirements below and start your monthly downpayment. I'll guide you through each document, even if you're abroad.

Please note: the reservation fee is non-refundable and non-transferable in case of withdrawal or cancellation, as stated in the developer's price list. Always pay the developer directly, never to a personal account.
